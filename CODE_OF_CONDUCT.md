# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what benefits the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized content, insults, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate this Code and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact mechanisms. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and respond proportionately.
